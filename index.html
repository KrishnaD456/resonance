How Living Ink Works

SPEED vs FORCE Forensic Ink Physics: Fast typing pulls letters closer together with a sleek -11° forward glide. Heavy force/pressure dumps 900-weight ink with a bottom nib-underline and thickens the bubble border.
CHURN Second Thoughts: 1.0x means you typed straight through. Higher numbers mean you rewrote your thought multiple times.
STARE Lingering: How many seconds you stared at the screen after typing before hitting Send.
LIWC-I Self-Focus: The percentage of "I, me, my" words—showing how personal or inward-focused the message is.
FORCE Nib Pressure: Measures key-hold duration on desktop and physical thumb-impact / shake G-force on mobile (up to 300ms).
MED-ITD & Q1 Pace & Burst: Your typical rhythm (MED-ITD) and fastest 25% of taps (Q1-FAST).
PAUSE Breathing Room: Hit Space and pause to grow a physical gap and ink pool between words.
BKSP Erase Energy: Calm leaves a neat single strike-through; Frantic furiously scratches the word out with a physical card shake.
